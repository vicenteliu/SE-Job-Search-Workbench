# Intents — the closed vocabulary

One line per intent: `name · family · what it probes`. Adding a line is a deliberate act; check the
aliases of existing cards first. Families: behavioural · technical · positioning · logistics.

## behavioural
- `conflict-with-manager` · behavioural · whether you can disagree upward and still deliver
- `conflict-with-peer` · behavioural · how you handle a colleague who is wrong, or thinks you are
- `failure-owned` · behavioural · whether you can name a real mistake and what changed after it
- `ambiguity` · behavioural · what you do when nobody can tell you what "done" looks like
- `priorities-under-load` · behavioural · how you choose when everything is urgent
- `influence-without-authority` · behavioural · getting a change made through people you do not manage
- `learning-fast` · behavioural · how you got competent at something under time pressure
- `feedback-received` · behavioural · whether you can take hard feedback and show what you did with it

## technical
- `troubleshooting-method` · technical · whether you have a method, or only experience
- `debug-under-pressure` · technical · the first ten minutes of an outage
- `design-tradeoff` · technical · can you name what you gave up, not just what you chose
- `automation-judgement` · technical · what you automate, what you refuse to, and why
- `scale-story` · technical · what broke when the numbers grew, and what you did
- `security-in-the-day-job` · technical · how you handle the security half of an ordinary task
- `migration-or-cutover` · technical · sequencing, blast radius, rollback
- `tool-you-have-not-run` · technical · the honest three-beat answer for something you know but have not operated
- `explain-to-a-non-engineer` · technical · whether you can drop the jargon without dropping the content

## positioning
- `self-intro` · positioning · who you are in sixty seconds, and the one thing to remember
- `why-this-company` · positioning · whether you read the JD and can say why this one
- `why-leaving` · positioning · the calm short version, and whether it stays calm under a follow-up
- `strengths-weaknesses` · positioning · whether the weakness is real and the strength is evidenced
- `where-in-five-years` · positioning · whether this role is a step or a stop
- `what-do-you-know-about-us` · positioning · reading, not guessing; an inference is asked, never asserted

## logistics
- `compensation` · logistics · the number, the range, and what you say before you have a number
- `availability-and-location` · logistics · start date, on-site, relocation, authorization
- `other-processes` · logistics · whether you are interviewing elsewhere and how you say so
