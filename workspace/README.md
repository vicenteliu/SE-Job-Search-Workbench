# Your workbench

The folder layout is the contract: the four skills read and write these paths and nothing else.

```
workbench/
  facts/
    master.md      your career as tagged facts — append-only, never edited to fit a job
    lexicon.md     the words you stop on out loud, with respellings
  stories/         four-beat story cards, one per tool or episode (TEMPLATE.md is the shape)
  jds/<slug>/      one folder per job: JD.md, then the teardown, talk track, grill, debrief
  techbank/        tech cards that stay true at the next company
  questions/       one card per intent; INTENTS.md is the closed vocabulary
```

Two rules:

- **Nothing here is written by a tool without you confirming it.** Skills propose; you confirm. A
  fact you did not confirm is not citable.
- **`jds/` is disposable, everything else accrues.** A teardown is wrong tomorrow and rerun; the
  question bank, the tech bank and the stories are what make the second interview cheaper than the first.

Start with `facts/master.md`: paste your resume in, split it into facts, tag each one honestly, and
confirm. That hour is the whole foundation.
