# Your resume against one JD

The comparison is a table, not a number.

| # | Requirement (JD's words) | Kind | Match | Fact | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Build and run a Linux provisioning pipeline | required | full | f012 | built one, ran it for years |
| 2 | Intune administration | required | partial | f031 | exposure only |
| 3 | Kubernetes in production | preferred | gap | closest: f040 | ran it in a lab |
| 4 | Active security clearance | hard filter | **unmet** | — | |

**Required: 1 full, 1 partial of 2 · Preferred: 0 of 1 · Hard filters: 1 of 1 unmet.**

- **One requirement per row**, in the JD's order, in the JD's words shortened. Splitting is the
  work: "maintain and troubleshoot the virtualization environment" is two requirements.
- **Kind.** `hard filter` is anything that ends a candidacy on its own — clearance, work
  authorization, a degree stated as required, an on-site location stated as required, a licence.
  It is counted apart, because "8 of 9" means nothing if the ninth is a hard no.
- **Match** is `full` (the fact answers it as asked), `partial` (adjacent: smaller scope, older, or an
  `EXPOSURE` fact), or a gap. A gap carries the closest fact you *do* have; that is what you will
  say instead of nothing.
- **Counts, never a percentage.** A count can be defended line by line. "78% match" is a figure
  with no line under it, and the first thing it does is get quoted to yourself.

## The tailored resume

A patch, not a rewrite: the same facts, reordered and re-emphasised for this JD, in the JD's
vocabulary where the fact genuinely supports it. Every bullet carries the id of the fact it came
from. A bullet that cites nothing does not get rendered. A requirement the facts do not cover is
left out of the resume and put in the gap report — it will come up in the interview, and that is
where it is handled, prepared.

## The gap report

The unmatched rows, each with the closest fact. It is the input to the next step: every gap becomes
a question you will be asked, and a question you will be asked deserves an answer prepared in
advance, not a blank ([04](04-questions-by-intent.md)).
