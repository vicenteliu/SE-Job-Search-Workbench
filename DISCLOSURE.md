# Disclosure

This repository is a one-way extraction from the author's private job-search workspace. The rule
of the extraction: **method crosses, content does not.**

## What crossed

- Four skills (`jd-teardown`, `question-bank`, `tech-card`, `talk-track`), rewritten so that
  every path points into *your* workspace and every reference to the author's own facts, stories,
  employers, interviews and decision records is gone.
- The workspace layout and the file formats (fact tags, question cards keyed by intent, tech cards
  with a retrieval surface, four-beat stories).
- The reasoning in `docs/`, written fresh for a reader who is not the author.
- The `intake` skill, written fresh for this repository: the public form of the intake step the
  author's own tooling runs (propose a tag, the user confirms, ask only what is missing, eight
  questions at most). Its interview format is borrowed from the author's own decision-grilling
  habit — numbered questions, a recommended answer each, one round at a time.

## What did not cross

- Any fact about the author's career, any employer name, any interview, any story, any question
  actually asked of him, any pronunciation note, any number.
- The author's private decision records. Where a rule here came from one, the rule is stated on its
  own and the record is not cited.
- The author's job-capture tooling, his content pipeline, and his reading console.
- **The live interview assistant.** The author's own rule for his own interviews is *rehearse only*;
  the tool records both sides of a call, and where it was built that needs everyone's consent. It is
  not in this repository, not sold with the setup, and not described in any listing.

## What the seller does and does not do

The setup service installs, tunes and walks through. It does not write. If a line of your resume
or your question bank was typed by the seller, the one rule of this workbench has already been
broken, and the seller has agreed not to break it.
